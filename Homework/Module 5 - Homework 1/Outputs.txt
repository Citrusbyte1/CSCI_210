Infix expression            ===> Postfix expression

  14                            ===> 14
  3 + 4 + 5                   ===> 3 4 + 5 +
  8 - 6 / 3 * 2              ===> 8 6 3 / 2 * -
  10 / 5 - 14 / 7           ===> 10 5 / 14 7 / -
  1 2 3 4 + &               ===> Error: Unexpected operator encountered: &
  ( 8 - 3 )                    ===> 8 3 -
  ( 4 + 2 ) * ( 9 / 3 )     ===> 4 2 + 9 3 / *
  5 * ( ( 4 + 2 ) / 3 )     ===> 5 4 2 + 3 / *
